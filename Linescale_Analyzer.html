Before each recording

  1. Check the setup. Enable onboard logging. Confirm the units, rate, trigger and recording times shown above.
  2. Set zero / tare. Follow your test method. Do not copy the sample CSV’s RelZero number.
  3. Arm the logger. Use the device’s LOG control and confirm it is ready. Single-log mode records once: arm it again for the next test.
  4. Check a trial file. Copy the CSV over USB. Verify its rate, trigger and capture time, then import it here.

These are recording presets for your workflow, not an ANSI Z359.13 test procedure. Menu wording varies by firmware. Device references: LineGrip quick-start / Log menu · How pre-capture works · Manuals by firmware

What do the numbers mean?
  • Peak: highest force in the file.
  • Recording: total recorded length, including pre-capture.
  • Dynamic average: average of samples at or above 500 lb.
  • Static hold: longest uninterrupted run at or above 5,000 lb.
  • No activity: no samples reached that folder’s threshold. Review before removing.
These are review metrics, not certification verdicts.
